Every word node is a ship in one of two warring fleets (failure-share ≥ 0.5 = coral-hulled failure fleet sailing from oil; success-share = mint-hulled success fleet seeking clean water; prompt-leaked words fly a gold diamond pennant); the ocean is pre-dyed by each word's failure share via a coarse 160×96 bias grid, and differential edges render as faint channel currents between ships. **Failure is oil, success is water**: users drag to spill oil (brush slider), which diffuses, drifts with the tide (flushed right at low tide, washed out left at high tide), and pollutes/slows success ships; **network connectivity is the tide enhancer** — hovering, pinning (click), or searching-and-locking (Enter) a word adds connection scaled by its graph degree, so hubs raise the tide fastest; high tide brightens the sea, swells the waves, scrubs oil, and accelerates the success fleet. Search spotlights and dims matches ("not in these waters" on a miss), hover tooltips show each word's trace stats, and periodic surge waves sweep the sea. - **Tides of the Trace — gameplay overhaul (debug pass + 9 new systems)** — the naval physics sandbox (`website/assets/html/tides-widget.html`) is rebuilt with advanced mechanics, still dependency-free and fully offline:
  - **Debug fixes** — spawns are now a deterministic fleet-stratified scatter (share-linear x placement and edge-spring blobs that piled ships into vertical stripes are gone; a damped repulsion relaxation plus a pairwise separation pass leaves zero overlaps at layout); clicking a ship while a search-lock is active now releases the lock *and* clears the search UI consistently; oil buffers are ping-ponged instead of reallocated every frame; oil advection is continuous through the tide threshold (no direction jitter) and surge bands are scrub-consistent; runtime grid repulsion keeps ships from overlapping during play (dragging included).
  - **Non-clustered spawns & dynamic map** — ships scatter deterministically across the whole sea (707 vessels, 0 overlaps), and a procedural hash-noise sea floor places shoals/reefs that slow ships, deflect the oil, and slow your drags; the terrain drifts subtly over time.
  - **Cooler water** — animated two-octave caustic shimmer, a moving sun-glint band, whitecap foam at wave crests on high chop, depth shading over the sea floor, plus fog, rain streaks, and lightning flashes (storm).
  - **Vessel sprites by network degree** — seven hand-drawn classes: sloop, schooner, frigate, destroyer, cruiser, battleship, aircraft carrier (flight deck + island + angled runway); size scales with degree, funnels smoke, moving ships leave wakes, pennants and leak diamonds retained.
  - **Sandbox drag** — press-drag any ship with pointer capture; it auto-pins (connection held) while moving, scrubs oil from its hull and wake, slows over shoals, and flings on release with retained momentum. Click-without-drag still toggles the pin.
  - **Signal shields** — battleships and carriers project pulsing shield rings scaled by degree and your current connection (bigger when pinned): oil inside is cleansed, friendly ships are steered to the shield, enemy drift is pushed away.
  - **Choppy tide** — a harmonic tide oscillator (17s–141s components) + storm swells rides on the connection target, so the tide visibly rises and falls; oil flushing is strongly non-linear above ~55% tide, so storm-surge + full connection washes the sea clean.
  - **More drift** — ambient time-evolving noise currents, a weather wind vector, per-ship gusts, and a tide-driven current, all scaled by the drift slider.
  - **Dynamic weather** — a calm → breeze → squall → storm state machine with eased transitions and connection-boosted storm odds; weather drives waves, drift, oil advection, fog, rain, lightning, and tide amplitude, with a live readout in the gauge and a `weather auto` toggle (off = force a state).

## [v1.0.0] - 2026-08-05

Initial beta release of the 16-class zero-shot document classification pipeline (versioned prompts v0–v17.2, OpenRouter eval runner, Braintrust manifests/scorers, deterministic dataset slices, cost/failure reporting). Tagged at commit `7daca5a`.

### Added

- **Manifest row tags (`OK` / `MISS!` / `ERROR!`)** — the eval runner stamps every manifest record with a `tag` (OK = correct, MISS! = wrong prediction, ERROR! = failed/empty) and prints `OK ` / `MISS!` / `ERROR!` labels in the per-image summary. `score_manifest.load_manifest()` derives the tag in-memory when absent, so pre-existing manifests still report tags; the 1120-image v11.8 manifest was rewritten with physical tags (1120 unique rows → 925 OK / 195 MISS! / 0 ERROR!).
- **`braintrust_report_manifest.py`** — manifest-first full report generator. Merges reasoning + metric traces from Braintrust experiments (matches the exact experiment name plus Braintrust's resume-loop version suffixes like `-06b91b68`) and writes `report_<exp>.md`, `per_class_accuracy_*.png`, `confusion_matrix_*.{png,md}`, `misclassification_reasoning_*.md` to `reports/`, appending a summary to `docs/experiments/experiment_log.md`. Reuses the chart/confusion/reasoning/cost helpers from `braintrust_report.py` and `load_manifest`/canonical-config handling from `score_manifest.py`. Guards against Braintrust span events whose `input` is a list, and reports expected-vs-actual cost including a prompt-caching note (cached input billed at ~10%).
- **Braintrust scorers: exact_match, failure, cost** — the Braintrust eval registers exactly three scorers: `exact_match` (exact-match accuracy), `failure` (error-sentinel rows), and `cost` (per-row actual billed USD from OpenRouter's `usage.cost`, captured by the runner and read by a code scorer — no price args needed). All three are code scorers (no LLM calls, no scorer credits). Near-miss (runner-up) is NOT a Braintrust scorer; it is computed locally by `score_manifest.py` from the `runner_up` the runner records in manifest + span metadata.
- **Local failure-rate + cost reporting in `score_manifest.py`** — the manifest now records a `cost` field per completed row (OpenRouter `usage.cost`); `score_manifest.py` reports `failure_rate`, `total_cost_usd`, `cost_coverage`, and `avg_cost_per_image_usd` in `_final.json`/`_final.md`, and backfills missing `cost` (like `runner_up`) from Braintrust span metadata (read-only fetch, no scorer credits). Guarantees failure/cost numbers are available locally even if Braintrust score/credit limits cap out.
- **Near-miss (runner-up) scoring** — `score_manifest.py` computes 1.0 when the model misclassifies but its stated runner-up label (the "Runner-up:" line the prompt forces into the reasoning trace) was the correct answer. It is a pure code parse (no LLM calls). `extract_runner_up()` lives in `src/openrouter_classifier.py`; the eval runner records `runner_up` in both the manifest and Braintrust span metadata.
- **Runner-up backfill for pre-tracking rows** — `score_manifest.py` now computes near-miss for the full run: rows recorded with a `runner_up` field count directly, and rows completed before runner-up tracking shipped are backfilled by parsing their reasoning traces from Braintrust experiment metadata (read-only fetch, no scorer credits; `--no-backfill` to skip). Final reports (`_final.json`/`_final.md`) now include near_miss count/accuracy, share-of-misses, runner-up coverage, and the near-miss row list. Backfill matches both the exact experiment name and Braintrust's version-suffixed names (`<name>-*`), and resolves config from `braintrust.env` as the source of truth even when `.env`'s stale key is already in the process env.
- **Faster experiment fetch** — `src/braintrust_utils.py` paginates `/experiment/{id}/fetch` with `limit=1000` (was 100), cutting the round-trips needed to read an experiment's spans by ~10x (validated against the API).

- `resume_until_complete.py` — re-invokes the eval runner from its manifest checkpoint until every dataset row has a final status, so a run persists to full completion even if the Braintrust side crashes or caps out. Alerts with the failure motif on eval-process crashes and on abandonment after `--max-cycles`.

- **HTTP timeout (300s)** added to the OpenAI client to prevent hung eval runs on stalled provider connections.
- **Manifest support** added to all eval launches for resumability after interruptions.
- **Resilient eval retry loop** — `braintrust_openrouter_input.py` now: (1) fails over to the alternate OpenRouter key (`RESEARCH_FUNDING_API_KEY`) when the primary hits a quota/credit 403, (2) throttles adaptively on upstream 429s (records recent rate-limits, sleeps per `Retry-After` or an escalating 5/15/45s backoff), (3) sleeps 2s/4s for other transient errors, (4) salvages rows that fail all primary retries with one attempt through `--fallback-model` (e.g. `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`) — designed for content-filtered images, and (5) logs span metadata safely (never raises on Braintrust logging failure). Per-error-type backoffs are module-level constants.
- **`--fallback-model` wired through `resume_until_complete.py`** — the resume watcher passes the salvage model through to each relaunched eval cycle, and `--no-score` disables its new default auto-scoring of the manifest on completion (via `score_manifest.py`, zero Braintrust credits).
- **Completion alert sounds** — the eval runner (`braintrust_openrouter_input.py`) now plays the success jingle at the end of a run with zero failed rows and the failure motif if any row errored (catchall for failures/errors in a Braintrust run). Mute with `--no-sound`.
